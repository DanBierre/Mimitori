The rules this sets

  • One accent, ink blue. Word state is shown by how a word is marked, not by colour: marker for new, dotted underline for in Anki, nothing for known.
  • Numbers link everything. The small number on a word in the line matches its row in the readout and its entry. Press 1–9 to jump.
  • Lexicon type: a serif for Japanese words and meanings, plain sans for the interface, and mono only for timecodes, counts and keys.
  • Instrument structure: the readout table, keyboard hints and exact timecodes. Rules, not boxes. A heavy rule opens each entry.
  • Light and dark follow the device. The Read sample is 走れメロス, which is in the public domain.
