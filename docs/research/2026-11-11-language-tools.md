# Language-learning tools: competitive research (2026-11-11)

Method: web searches only, no direct Reddit access (Reddit mirrors only). Many "review" sources are competitor or affiliate marketing; those are flagged **[biased]**. Anything not directly supported is marked **[uncertain]**.

## Per-tool findings

### Language Reactor
- **Loved:** Anki-focused Netflix/YouTube watching; called the "gold standard" by a competitor comparison [biased]. https://www.trancy.org/blog/7-best-language-reactor-alternatives-compared-2026-3609d2252005811db7f7d41131756428
- **Complaints:** desktop-only, no mobile app; Pro about $5/month [biased]. Forum threads show Japanese gaps (furigana on hover, blur translation not working for Japanese, romaji request). https://forum.languagelearningwithnetflix.com/ A forum reviewer argues some features work against effective Japanese study (unspecified). https://forum.languagelearningwithnetflix.com/t/my-language-reactor-review/13008 Breakage after Chrome updates: low-authority source **[uncertain]**.
- **Steal:** hover-to-reveal / blur-translation as a gentle "try first, check later" mode.

### Migaku
- **Loved:** subtitle lookup plus one-flow card creation (sentence, audio, screenshot) on Netflix/YouTube; monolingual dictionaries; local video with own subtitles; comprehension score to pick content. https://www.tofugu.com/japanese-learning-resources-database/migaku/ . "Best Paid Solution" and "Best Video Player" in the 2025 community awards: https://skerritt.blog/best-japanese-learning-tools-2025-award-show/
- **Complaints:** price (about $7-10/month per competitor posts [biased]; figures conflict); Trustpilot reviewer expected "much higher" quality for the price (June 2025); Android reviews cite crashes, locked features, bugs (aggregator summary, 4.19 from 96 ratings). https://it.trustpilot.com/review/migaku.com ; https://android-stats.com/d/com.migaku.android/reviews . No kanji/radical teaching.
- **Steal:** a pre-watch comprehension % for a video, and highlighting "good sentences to mine".

### LingQ
- **Loved:** extensive-reading workflow; large content library (general reviews). https://www.alllanguageresources.com/lingq-review/
- **Complaints:** Japanese AI translations called unusable; platform "worse after AI" (Trustpilot). https://jp.trustpilot.com/review/lingq.com Annual-discount wording called misleading by one reviewer (allegation). About $15/month **[uncertain]**.
- **Steal:** a lesson rather than a pattern: never show machine translation without a reliability cue.

### Yomitan + asbplayer
- **Loved:** Yomitan won "Best Overall" (desktop and mobile browsers, Anki, audio); asbplayer is the best free subtitle player. https://skerritt.blog/best-japanese-learning-tools-2025-award-show/ . asbplayer adds sentence and audio capture to cards. https://docs.asbplayer.dev/docs/guides/one-click-mining
- **Friction:** setup is a multi-tool stack: install Yomitan, import dictionaries, install Anki + AnkiConnect, match the note type in both tools, enable file-URL access. https://learnjapanese.moe/mining/ ; https://docs.asbplayer.dev/docs/guides/one-click-mining . Guides date from different years and drift.
- **Steal:** one-click mine with sentence + audio clip; zero-account feel.

### jpdb.io
- **Loved:** deck/text coverage metric and frequency ranking; strict, fast SRS. https://kampffrosch94.github.io/posts/jpdb_faq
- **Complaints:** coverage vs vocabulary counts confuse users (unique words vs every occurrence); review-pile burnout thread (2023); scheduling does not transfer to other tools (per Jiten). https://jiten.moe/guides/jpdb-alternative ; https://kampffrosch94.github.io/posts/jpdb-year-review
- **Steal:** "You know 87% of this text" before reading, with the % defined in plain words.

### Anki / AnkiMobile
- **Loved:** power, free on desktop/Android/web, huge ecosystem; one App Store reviewer calls the $25 well spent. https://www.gwern.net/docs/www/itunes.apple.com/bec5c28de269072dd3c44073a67e7c411e7d0757.html
- **Complaints:** iOS app about $25 (regional prices vary); UI described as dated and unintuitive; steep first-deck learning curve. https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-improve-ankimobile-s-interface-design/67315 ; https://nibble-app.com/blog/anki-review
- **Steal:** be the friendly front door. Own the simple review UI and send cards to Anki for people who want it.

### WaniKani
- **Loved:** mnemonics plus SRS; well-documented API; $9/mo, $89/yr, $299 lifetime. https://www.mezzoguild.com/wanikani-review
- **Complaints:** "ugly and overwhelming" dashboard, unclear next steps; review pile-ups after breaks; no listening/speaking. Same source.
- **Steal:** a pause option and a daily-count cap; avoid its cluttered dashboard.

### Renshuu
- **Loved:** Best Phone App in 2025 community awards; adapts to level, mixes kanji, flashcards, games. https://skerritt.blog/best-japanese-learning-tools-2025-award-show/
- **Complaints:** one forum user called its UI the worst in 10 years, "bloated, buggy". https://community.wanikani.com/t/does-anybody-use-renshuu/54938 (single opinion; another user praised customisation).
- **Steal:** deep customisation hidden behind sane defaults.

### Satori Reader
- **Loved:** graded, native-written stories with tap-for-grammar notes; Tofugu: likely "perfect" for upper-beginner to intermediate; no ads. https://www.tofugu.com/reviews/satori-reader/ ; https://blog.satorireader.com/2024/10/30/why-satori-reader-is-not-ad-supported
- **Complaints:** paywalled ($8.99/mo, $89.99/yr US App Store); SRS weaker than dedicated tools (Tofugu). https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/satori-reader/id1382950847
- **Steal:** audio plus text, in-line grammar notes, calm reading layout.

### Bunpro
- **Loved:** grammar SRS; strong customisation. https://www.tofugu.com/reviews/bunpro
- **Complaints:** busy UI; "not as addictive" as WaniKani; sessions can feel repetitive. https://community.bunpro.jp/t/is-it-me-or-is-bunpro-just-not-as-addictive-as-wanikani/20449?page=2
- **Steal:** link a saved word's example sentence to the grammar point in it **[idea, not sourced]**.

### Duolingo (UX polish only)
- **Works:** mascot in feedback and error states, streak, confetti, progress rings, motion tied to progress. https://madegooddesigns.com/duolingo-brand-guidelines/ ; https://www.925studios.co/blog/duolingo-design-breakdown . Critics say engagement design can outweigh teaching. Motion timings in third-party token files are unofficial **[uncertain]**.
- **Steal:** small, rewarding micro-feedback on save/review. Skip streak guilt.

### Newcomers worth knowing
- **Jiten** (free, open source): per-title difficulty scores, frequency-ordered decks, coverage from your known words, built-in SRS, jpdb import, browser reader extension. "Best Website" in awards. https://jiten.moe/guides/what-is-jiten ; https://github.com/Sirush/Jiten
- **Manabi Reader** (iOS): native reader with Anki export and comprehension stats; "Best iOS app" 2025; Yomitan dictionary support unclear **[uncertain]**. https://www.tofugu.com/japanese-learning-resources-database/manabi-reader/
- **Jidoujisho** (Android): Best Android app; mining from video, books, manga. https://skerritt.blog/best-japanese-learning-tools-2025-award-show/
- **AI tutors** (Speak, Hanashi etc.): no learner ratings found **[uncertain]**; not Mimitori's lane.

## Whisper note
No independent benchmarks of in-browser Whisper for Japanese found. Known failure: invented text on silence or music; VAD and silence trimming reduce it (WhisperJAV FAQ, self-reported). https://meizhong986.github.io/WhisperJAV/faq/ . Larger models are better for Japanese (vendor claim **[uncertain]**).

## Visual takeaways

**What well-regarded tools look like:**
- **Reading-first apps (Satori Reader, Manabi Reader):** one clear text column, generous spacing, no ads. Little direct style evidence found, so this is inferred **[uncertain]**.
- **Duolingo:** high contrast, big rounded buttons, one primary action per screen, short purposeful motion, character-led feedback. https://www.925studios.co/blog/duolingo-design-breakdown
- **Anki/Yomitan fans accept plain UI because it is fast.** Speed counts as design. https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/suggestion-improve-ankimobile-s-interface-design/67315

**Category clichés to avoid:**
- Cartoon mascots and streak-guilt flames (Duolingo clone look).
- Red/white "Japan" palette, rising sun, torii, sakura petals, brush-stroke fonts.
- Dense dashboards with a dozen stat tiles (WaniKani/Renshuu complaints).
- Hacker-dark Anki-style themes as a feature.
- XP and leaderboards that overshadow reading.

**Do instead (recommendation, not sourced):** a readable Japanese typeface sized for reading, restrained palette with one accent, motion only on save/review feedback, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
