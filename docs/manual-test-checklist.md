# Mimitori: hands-on test checklist

The automated run covers clicks, keys, layouts and the Anki sync logic. This list covers what only a person can judge: real files, real Anki cards, your phone and how it all feels.

Keep a note open while you test. Write down anything that breaks, and anything that just annoys you.

Use the Anki deck `Test` for every Anki step. Never use `日本語一般で` while testing.

## 1. A real episode (about 20 minutes)

1. Open Watch and drop in one of your own videos with its Japanese `.srt` or `.ass` file.
2. Add an English subtitle file too.
3. Watch a few minutes. Check that the line keeps time with the speech.
4. If it drifts, open **Subtitle timing** and use **Line up a line**.
5. Press `1`–`9` to pick words. Check that the numbers on the line match the readout table.
6. Check the readings and meanings for a few words with more than one reading (今日, 行く, 上手).
7. Turn on **Pause after each line** and watch a scene with it.
8. Try **Focus mode** (`F`) and get back out with `Esc`.

## 2. Make subtitles (about 15 minutes, a big download the first time)

1. Drop in a short video without subtitles.
2. Click **Make subtitles** and let the speech model download (about 560 MB).
3. Check how long it takes, and how good the Japanese lines are.
4. Try **Stop**, then **Carry on**.
5. Fix one line with the pencil in the transcript.

## 3. A real manga and a real PDF (about 15 minutes)

1. Open a manga chapter (CBZ or a folder of pages) in Read.
2. Box a speech bubble. The first time, the text reader downloads (about 117 MB).
3. Check the text it reads, for both vertical and horizontal bubbles.
4. Turn pages both ways. Check that right-to-left feels right.
5. Open a PDF with real text, like a novel or an article. Box a paragraph and check the text.

## 4. Anki with your real note type (about 15 minutes)

1. Open Anki.
2. In Mimitori Settings, connect, pick the deck `Test`, and click **Check fields**.
3. Check that the field matching looks right for your note type.
4. Turn on **Send saves to Anki**.
5. In Watch, add a word with `A`.
6. Open the card in Anki. Check the word, the reading, the meaning, the sentence, the screenshot and the audio.
7. In Mimitori, press **I know this** on a word that's in Anki. Check that the card is suspended in Anki.
8. Unsuspend it in Anki, then click **Sync known words** in Mimitori. Check that the word shows as "in Anki" again.

## 5. Your phone (about 10 minutes)

1. Open Mimitori on your phone, either through the install button or the dev server on your network.
2. Load the sample clip. Tap words, then save one.
3. Scroll the transcript. Check that the video stays pinned at the top.
4. Read some pasted text. Check that the word sheet doesn't cover the word you tapped.
5. Switch light and dark.

## 6. Odds and ends (about 5 minutes)

1. Save a backup in Settings, change something, then restore the backup.
2. Switch the interface to 日本語 and read through each screen for clumsy wording.
3. Review a few words in the Review tab with Anki turned off.
